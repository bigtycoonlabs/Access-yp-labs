# Why We Built Access YP Labs

### A white paper from Set Up Your Place LLC

*Revised 7 August 2026. Every figure in this paper describes the platform as it actually works today.*

---

## A different kind of front door

Most people have had an idea for a business at some point. A better version of something they use every day. A service their town is missing. A thing they'd buy in a heartbeat if only someone made it.

And most of those ideas die in the same place: a notes app, a napkin, the back of someone's mind. Not because the idea was bad — but because the distance between *having* an idea and *doing something* with it is enormous. It usually takes money you don't have, time you can't spare, connections you were never given, and the nerve to bet it all before you have any proof it will work.

We built Access YP Labs to shrink that distance to almost nothing.

---

## Who we are

Set Up Your Place LLC is a small company with a stubborn belief: **the tools that decide who gets to build a business have been handed to the wrong people for too long.** Access, capital, know-how, a fair shot — these have always flowed to the people who already had them.

We know that from the inside. This company was founded by entrepreneurs who have lived what it means to be underestimated and locked out — to be handed tools that simply weren't made with you in mind. So we build the opposite. Everything we make is built to be used by everyone, on purpose, from the first line of code — designed to work beautifully for people who can't see a screen, and for anyone the usual systems overlook. Accessibility isn't a feature we add at the end. It's the reason we exist.

Access YP Labs is one of a small family of platforms we're building under that same idea — each one opening a different door that used to be closed. Labs is the one about **ideas, and the chance to turn them into something real.**

## What Access YP Labs actually is

Here's the simplest way to say it.

Access YP Labs is a place where you take an idea for a business and turn it into a real, finished plan — with the help of an AI partner named Clay — and then you get to choose what happens next. You can build it out and launch it yourself. Or you can sell it, like an asset, to someone who will. Or you can help sell *other people's* ideas and earn money doing it.

It's a marketplace for businesses that haven't been born yet. We call the ideas that live there **dreams**, and the marketplace **the Dream Market**, because that's honestly what they are — the businesses the world never launched, finally given a shelf, a price, and a chance.

A few things make it work:

- **You start with nothing but a spark.** You don't need a plan, or money, or a degree. You tell Clay an idea — even a half-formed one — or you ask Clay to find one for you.
- **Clay does the hard part with you.** Clay shapes your idea into a complete project: a real plan, honest research about whether people actually want it, a clear path to build it, and an honest look at the risks. Clay will tell you when something is hard. Clay will never make up a number to make you feel good.
- **You prove it before you bet on it.** You can put up a simple page to see if real people want the thing — before you spend a dollar building it. Proof, not hope.
- **You get a real site, not a mock-up.** Clay builds the project a website with actual pages, a free address, and a storefront that can take real money — into your own account, with us taking nothing from those sales.
- **You don't have to do it alone.** The Launch Partner board is where you say what help you need, or offer what you're good at. You agree your own terms directly. We take no cut of that and we are not party to it.
- **Then you decide.** Keep it and launch it. Sell it. Or set it down. It's yours, and it's private, until *you* choose otherwise. Nothing is ever posted or sold without you saying so — and no one on our staff can read a private project, including us.

## What it costs, and how we make money

We'll say this plainly, because a paper that explains its philosophy and hides its pricing isn't being straight with you.

**Building with Clay is free and unlimited.** Shape as many ideas as you like, for as long as you like, and pay nothing. **Your first project is free forever** — built, added to, downloaded, taken off the platform entirely, listed and sold, at no cost. Everything beyond that is **one plan at $19 a month**: unlimited projects, the website builder, the storefront, the images Clay makes as he works.

When something sells in the Dream Market, **we take 20% and you keep 80%** — on every marketplace sale, however the buyer got there. When you sell through your project's own website, direct to your own customers, **we take nothing at all**.

And a Dream Mover — someone who promotes a project they believe in — earns 5% of a sale they drove. That comes out of our share, never out of the seller's.

We'd rather tell you the uncomfortable part too. Most things listed in most marketplaces don't sell. On Gumroad the median seller earns around $72 a month and nearly half of all listed products earn nothing at all. We will not be different at the start, and anyone who tells you a listing is a payday is selling you something. That's exactly why the free tier is genuinely free and why the plan has to be worth it on its own merits — if the only thing justifying $19 is a sale that may never come, the price is wrong.

---

And there's one line we never cross. The Dream Market sells *ideas* — everything right up to the moment an idea becomes a real, running, licensed business. Once it's alive and making money for real customers, it's not a dream anymore. It's a company. And a company belongs to the person who built it.

## Why Clay exists

Clay isn't a chatbot we bolted on because AI is popular. **Clay is here because this specific place needed him.**

Turning a rough idea into a real, honest, buildable business plan is genuinely hard work — the kind that normally costs a consultant's fee, or years of learning, or both. For most people that work never gets done, and the idea dies for lack of a partner. We wanted to hand everyone that partner. One who is patient, who explains the rules of the game, who is honest to a fault, and who works just as well for someone listening with a screen reader as for anyone else.

Clay does considerably more than write a plan. He researches the live web and cites real sources. He builds a clickable prototype you can actually use. He writes the website and the storefront. He'll tell you what your project is honestly worth as a listing, and why. And when what you need is real software, he writes the complete build spec — every screen, the data, the rules, the services and their real costs — for you to hand to a developer or paste into a tool like Claude Code or Lovable.

That last one is a deliberate limit rather than a gap. We don't build or host applications, and we're not going to pretend otherwise. What we're good at is understanding a business well enough to say precisely what needs building. So we write that document, hand it to you, and it's yours to take anywhere.

That's Clay. He was built on purpose, for this exact gap — the space between a person with an idea and a person who's ever built anything. He's a coach, a co-builder, and a straight shooter. The most important thing about him is the thing he *won't* do: he won't lie to you. He won't invent a statistic or promise you'll get rich. In a world full of tools that tell you what you want to hear, we built one that tells you the truth — because when you're deciding whether to bet your time and money on something, the truth is the most valuable thing anyone can give you.

## We write in public, too

Clay writes a public library — The Desk — about the actual work of starting something very small: pricing, finding your first customers, what to hand off, when to raise prices. And once a week he writes **Clay Weekly**, a short magazine: the five pieces worth your time, one business term explained properly, what changed out there for very small operations, and whoever quietly kept turning up that week — not who sold something. Presence, not achievement.

Nothing Clay writes publishes itself. Every piece waits for a person to read and approve it.

There's a point of view in that magazine we'll state here as well. An idea nobody has built is treated by every serious institution as worth precisely nothing. Banks won't lend against it. Investors won't look until it already works. The entire business press writes about companies that already made it, as though the interesting part were the end rather than the beginning. That isn't a judgement about ideas — it's a failure of imagination, and a gap so obvious it's almost funny it was left sitting there. An unbuilt business that's been researched properly, costed honestly, and shaped so someone else could pick it up and run is **an asset**. It has value the moment it exists, not the moment it succeeds. We're simply the first people to treat it that way and write the price on the tag.

## The mission, plainly

**We want to make the chance to build a business something everyone can reach.**

Not just people with savings. Not just people with the right connections or the right schooling or the right eyesight. Everyone. We want a person with nothing but a good idea and an internet connection to be able to shape it, prove it, own it, and either launch it or sell it — and to earn real money along the way, in whatever role fits their life.

That's it. That's the whole thing. Everything on the platform — the honest AI, the marketplace, the ways to earn, the accessibility built into every corner — points at that one goal.

We hope Access YP Labs becomes the place the world starts.

We hope that when someone, anywhere, has an idea they can't shake, this is where they bring it — the way people bring their money to a bank or their questions to a search engine. That's the company we're trying to be, and Access YP Labs is where we're trying hardest to prove it.

If you've got an idea you can't stop thinking about, this was built for you. Come find out what it's worth.
