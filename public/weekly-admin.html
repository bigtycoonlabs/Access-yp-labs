Clay Weekly — the newsroom

Where an issue gets made. Clay assembles it; you decide what happens next. The four steps run in order, and each one is separate on purpose — publishing does not email anybody, and sending is its own deliberate act.

Step one — assemble this week's draft

Clay gathers what actually happened this week — the pieces he wrote, who listed a project, who earned as a Dream Mover — and writes the opening and his Note. Safe to run more than once: it rebuilds the same week's draft rather than making a second one, and it will not touch an issue you already approved.

Step two — choose a Project of the Week

Projects where somebody is clearly taking it somewhere: listed in the Dream Market, has a working site, or the creator told us they are building or launching. Asking sends that creator an email from Clay with accept and decline links. Nobody is featured without saying yes, and if they decline, Clay never asks about that project again.

Step three and four — approve, publish, send

Approve the content, publish it to make the public page live, and only then send it by email. Sending happens once — an issue can never go out twice — and the count you get back is how many the mail provider actually accepted, not how many we hoped for.
