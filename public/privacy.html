Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026  |  Set Up Your Place LLC  |  1150 NW 72nd Avenue, Tower I, Suite 455, Miami, FL 33126

This Privacy Policy explains how Set Up Your Place LLC ("Set Up Your Place," "we," "us," or "our"), located at 1150 NW 72nd Avenue, Tower I, Suite 455, Miami, FL 33126, collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use Access YP Labs at accessyplabs.com, together with Clay and the Dream Market (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account information, such as your name or dreamer tag and email address, and a securely hashed version of your password.
  • Your Content, such as the ideas, prompts, and projects you create or refine with Clay.
  • Payment information you enter to subscribe or purchase, which is collected and processed by our payment processor, Stripe. We do not store your full card number.
  • Communications you send us, such as support requests.

Information collected automatically:

  • A visitor token and a one-way hashed (not plain) version of your IP address, used to recognize a returning visitor and show a simple visit count. We do not store your raw IP address for this feature.
  • Basic technical and usage data, such as your browser and device type and how you interact with the Service, collected through essential cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including building and refining projects with Clay;
  • process payments and manage subscriptions;
  • communicate with you, including about your account, projects, and support;
  • personalize your experience and remember your preferences;
  • maintain the security and integrity of the Service and prevent misuse; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

3. Artificial-Intelligence Processing

To generate projects, the prompts and content you submit to Clay are sent to third-party artificial-intelligence providers that power the Service. Those providers process the content to return a result and handle it under their own terms and privacy practices. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information you would not want processed in this way.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service, such as Stripe (payments), our AI providers (project generation), and our email provider, under obligations to protect the information.
  • The marketplace: if you choose to list a project, the project and its previews are shown to other members and prospective buyers, and buyer or seller details necessary to complete a transaction are shared with the counterparty.
  • Legal and safety reasons, such as to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety, and property of Set Up Your Place, our members, or others.
  • Business transfers, such as in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, to remember the returning-visitor token described above, and to keep the Service working. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling essential cookies may affect how the Service functions.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, and thereafter as needed to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you delete Your Content or close your account, we remove it from active use, except for reasonable residual backups and anything you have already shared, listed, or sold. You may request deletion by contacting us at success@accessyourplace.com.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information, including hashing passwords and encrypting data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can review and update your account information, adjust your preferences (including quieting reminders), and request access to or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at success@accessyourplace.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law, such as the right to access, correct, delete, or port your information, or to object to certain processing; we will honor those rights as required by law. You can opt out of non-essential emails using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to, and is not intended for, anyone under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Accessibility

We are committed to making the Service, including this Policy, accessible. If you use assistive technology and encounter a barrier, please contact us at success@accessyourplace.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will take reasonable steps to notify you, such as by updating the effective date above or by email. Your continued use of the Service after the changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.
