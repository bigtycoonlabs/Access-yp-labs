What she does

She keeps everything you owe in one place
Filings, licences, renewals, invoices, and the promises you made a customer. Ordered by what missing each one would cost you, with a reminder before it is due rather than after.
She builds what your business needs
A website, a customer portal your customers sign into, or a custom app. You see a working version first, free, and only pay once you have seen it and said yes. An app goes onto your own GitHub, in your account, so you can take it and leave whenever you like.
She checks the law for you, and shows her working
Ask what your business needs to stay legal where it operates and she reads the government websites herself, then gives you the answer with every page she used. When she cannot confirm something she says so. She never guesses.
She holds your files, keys and people
Documents, photos and credentials in one place, shared only with the people you choose, each seeing their own part and nothing else.
Money goes to Arbo, and she hands it over
The money side lives in YP Flow with Arbo. Connect the two and an invoice Penny is holding lands in your books there, waiting for you to confirm it.
You can talk to her
Type, or hold the button and speak. She answers out loud and keeps talking while she works, so you are never left wondering whether anything is happening.

Who it is for

A cleaner. A food truck. A furnished rental operator. An e-commerce brand. A consultant. One person, or up to about twenty.

What they have in common is not the trade. It is that the owner is the integration layer — the person holding the books, the bookings, the customers, the filings and the website together in their own head at eleven at night. Everything here exists to take that off them.

What she will not do

  • She will not invent a filing, a fee or a deadline. If she could not confirm it, she says so instead of guessing.
  • She will not tell you nothing is due when she could not check. A failed look is not a clear day, and she says which it was.
  • She does not do your books. That is Arbo, in YP Flow, and she hands you over rather than half-answering.
  • She will not claim she did something she did not do. What she says happened is what the tools actually saved.
